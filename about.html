Our Vision & Mission

Vision

To create inclusive, resilient, and future-ready communities where children, youth, and women are empowered with education, skills, health awareness, and climate resilience to thrive in the Age of AGI.

  • Expand equitable access to quality education
  • Build AGI-age skills and digital capabilities
  • Strengthen women’s economic participation
  • Improve community health outcomes
  • Promote environmental sustainability and climate resilience

All interventions are aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Mission

To design and implement high-impact, scalable, and measurable development programs that:

  • Expand equitable access to quality education
  • Build AGI-age skills and digital capabilities
  • Strengthen women’s economic participation
  • Improve community health outcomes
  • Promote environmental sustainability and climate resilience

All interventions are aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Why Pakistan | Why Now

(Evidence-Based Context)

64% Youth Population

64% of Pakistan’s population is under the age of 30 (World Bank)

Education Crisis

Over 22.8 million children are out of school — second-highest globally (UNICEF)

Climate Vulnerability

Climate change costs Pakistan ~9% of GDP annually (World Bank, 2022)

Gender Gap

Women’s labor force participation remains below 23%

Future of Work

40–60% of jobs will be redefined by AI & AGI by 2040 (OECD)

BES addresses these systemic risks through integrated, future-focused interventions.
